# Chinese sources and earlier exhibition records — 27 November 2026

## Scope and editorial decisions

Follow-up research searched both 龚明金 and 龔明金, English exhibition titles, Chinese newspaper domains, organiser archives, gallery catalogues and library records. Search results about unrelated people with the same Chinese name were excluded. Some newspaper searches were unavailable or returned no matching content; a successful HTTP response was not treated as proof that a page contained an article.

No additional complete Chinese newspaper interview was verified. Four new Chinese-first bilingual summaries were written from verified organiser, catalogue and institutional records. They are labelled as new editorial summaries, not original historical Chinese articles.

## New articles

- **Nanyangism 2007:** Harmoni–Soka's Chinese exhibition archive records 南洋风 2007 南洋美专（马）校友会美展 and the date 23 September 2007. Younie Gallery's Chinese artist profile and ArtSafe supply the artist's participation and Kuala Lumpur venue. No closing date is supplied.
- **Scenery of China Painting Exhibition 2008 by Malaysian Artists:** Galeri Seni Mutiara's anniversary catalogue, page 34, exhibition 44, explicitly lists Keng Seng Choo and 17–22 October 2008. The table does not specify a venue; no venue or exhibition in China is inferred from its title.
- **Penang collection catalogue:** National Gallery Singapore's Rotunda Library catalogue lists Keng Seng Choo in the contents of Penang State Art Gallery permanent collection 1965–2011 (304 pages; ISBN 9789834237677). This is a library/bibliographic record, not evidence that National Gallery Singapore owns his painting. Artwork details and acquisition dates remain unspecified.
- **Wells lot 071:** The 20 August 2023 auction catalogue, printed/PDF page 79, records a 1980 work as 水墨、纸本、镜框, 92 × 54 cm. No artwork title or completed sale is inferred. The one embedded artwork image was extracted intact, reviewed visually against the page, optimised to WebP and credited to its catalogue. No full catalogue page or unrelated material is republished.

## Earlier exhibitions

Twenty-four year-only entries from 1966–2007 were added to a separately labelled section of `/exhibitions/`, with source links on each entry. Younie's Chinese artist profile is the primary retrospective source; ArtSafe corroborates entries it lists. Years are not converted to fictional 1 January event dates. These entries are presented as gallery-biography records, not contemporary press reports. The sometimes inconsistent Taegu biennale dates were not added.

## Existing material strengthened

- The original 2012 Northern Malaysian Scenes newspaper article gains dates 14 October–4 November 2012, supported by the gallery catalogue's page 41, exhibition 114. Original text and publication date remain intact.
- The original 2010 北马风情展, 2009 Tainan exhibition and Garis-Garis Penghayatan announcement gain their explicitly recorded exhibition dates. The timeline now includes dated press records about exhibitions without changing their press classification or creating duplicate articles.
- The 2022 Malaysia–Korea record gains the organiser's Chinese-language source.
- The Town Hall 画缘 archive gains its current organiser reference. The current source gives 28–31 December without an explicit year; the old article gives 27–31 December. The source's displayed 12 January 2016 publication date is not used as the event year. The discrepancy is noted, and no event dates are asserted from it.
