[![Keng Seng Choo 龚明金](/media/art-cfea3d50c67d4d00.webp "Keng Seng Choo 龚明金")](/media/art-cfea3d50c67d4d00.webp)Keng Seng Choo is a full-time artist born in 1945 in Alor Setar, Kedah where he now lives with his wife. Their home houses a personal collection of his artworks spanning a career of over 45 years.

Keng Seng Choo graduated in 1965 from the Nanyang Academy of Fine Arts in Singapore. His works have won acclaim and many awards including a Silver Medal at the New York International Art Show in 1970. Many of his paintings are in public and institutional collection in Malaysia and overseas.

龚明金先生是一位来自马来西亚的全职画家。他于1945年出生在马来西亚吉打州，育有两男一女，如今和妻子住在吉打州亚罗士打县。他们的房子收藏了这位画家超过45年职业生涯的作品。

龚明金先生1965年毕业于新加坡南洋艺术学院。他的作品于1970年在纽约国际艺术展初试啼声赢得银奖后，多年来陆续在世界各地获得了好评和奖项。许多马来西亚和海外的公众和机构也收藏了他的作品。
