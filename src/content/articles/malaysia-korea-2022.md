The organiser’s record names Keng Seng Choo among the Malaysian participants in **Celebration of Harmony through Arts: Malaysia & Korea Art Exhibition – Kuala Lumpur 2022**.

The physical exhibition ran from **10 September to 9 October 2022** at Wisma Kebudayaan Soka Gakkai Malaysia. It marked 62 years of diplomatic relations between Malaysia and South Korea, presenting 57 artworks by 41 artists from the two countries.

This source documents his participation in the exchange. The organiser’s featured artwork selection does not identify which painting he contributed.
