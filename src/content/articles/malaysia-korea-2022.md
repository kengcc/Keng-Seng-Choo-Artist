Keng Seng Choo participated in **Celebration of Harmony through Arts: Malaysia & Korea Art Exhibition – Kuala Lumpur 2022**, held at Wisma Kebudayaan Soka Gakkai Malaysia from **10 September to 9 October 2022**.

Marking 62 years of diplomatic relations between Malaysia and South Korea, the exhibition brought together **57 artworks by 41 artists** from the two countries.

## 中文

龚明金参与了「Celebration of Harmony through Arts: Malaysia & Korea Art Exhibition – Kuala Lumpur 2022」，展期为2022年9月10日至10月9日，地点为吉隆坡马来西亚创价学会综合文化中心。

展览纪念马来西亚与韩国建交62周年，汇集两国41位艺术家的57件作品。
