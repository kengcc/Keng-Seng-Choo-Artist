Keng Seng Choo participated in **When the South Wind Is Blowing / 当南风吹起…**, held at **Younie Gallery** from **15 to 28 March 2026**.

The exhibition brought together works by graduates of Singapore’s **Nanyang Academy of Fine Arts**, where he completed his studies in 1965.
