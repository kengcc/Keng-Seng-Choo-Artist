KUALA LUMPUR, Sun. - Artist Keng Seng Choo has a spiritual message for people - be at peace, and he is delivering his message through an exhibition titled _Pigeons: A Celebration of Joy and Peace._

The exhibition will feature 50 oil paintings, 30 of them on pigeons.

"Pigeons are gentle and humane creatures ... people who lead hectic and stressful lives should adopt the pigeons' relaxed, carefree nature," he said.
