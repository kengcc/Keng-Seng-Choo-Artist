On **25 December 2019**, Oriental Daily published a report on the **迎春艺术家作品联展**, organised by City Art Gallery at Li Chi Mao Art Museum. The group exhibition brought together more than 160 artists and continued until **5 January 2020**.

The report includes a reproduction captioned as an oil painting by Keng Seng Choo. It provides an independent press reference for his work in the exhibition; the painting’s title, creation year and dimensions are not supplied.

The article’s interviews are with **谢文川** and **杨应平**, rather than Keng Seng Choo. Read the linked original for the wider exhibition coverage.

## 中文

《东方日报》于2019年12月25日刊登「迎春艺术家作品联展　汇集本地艺术佳作」，介绍连城画廊在李奇茂书画馆举办、展至2020年1月5日的联展。报道配有注明为「龚明金油画佳作」的作品图片，并未列出该画的画名、创作年份或尺寸。
