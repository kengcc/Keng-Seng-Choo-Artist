Keng Seng Choo’s work featured in **迎春艺术家作品联展**, a New Year group exhibition organised by City Art Gallery at Li Chi Mao Art Museum. The exhibition brought together more than **160 artists** and continued until **5 January 2020**.

**Oriental Daily’s 25 December 2019 coverage** illustrated one of his oil paintings alongside its report on the exhibition. Read the original article for the wider exhibition coverage and interviews with **谢文川** and **杨应平**.
