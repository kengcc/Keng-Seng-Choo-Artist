这件<strong>1980年</strong>的纸本作品，收录于 Wells <strong>2023年8月20日 Asian Fine Art & Premium Tea Auction</strong>图录，为<strong>拍品071</strong>。

图录第<strong>79页</strong>将创作年份记为<strong>「庚申 1980年」</strong>，媒材标为<strong>「水墨 纸本 镜框」</strong>，尺寸为<strong>92 × 54厘米</strong>，未列作品名称。

## English

This <strong>1980 work on paper</strong> by Keng Seng Choo appears as <strong>lot 071</strong> in Wells’ <strong>20 August 2023 Asian Fine Art & Premium Tea Auction</strong> catalogue.

On printed page <strong>79</strong>, the catalogue dates the work to <strong>1980</strong>, gives its medium and presentation as <strong>水墨、纸本、镜框</strong>, and records dimensions of <strong>92 × 54 cm</strong>. The work is listed without a title.
