Keng Seng Choo is the subject of a chapter in **心路2 — 走向大马艺术家**, a collection of interviews by **朵拉**, published by **Soka Gakkai Malaysia in 2011**.

The publisher’s table of contents lists the chapter **“终身的事业，幸福的意义 — 油画家龚明金”**. The book’s ISBN is **978-967-1046-51-7**.

This entry records the publication and chapter confirmed by the publisher. It does not reproduce the interview, whose full text has not been reviewed for this website.

For another conversation with the same writer already preserved in this archive, read [For Art, No Regrets / 因为艺术，无悔无憾的龚明金](/articles/for-art-no-regrets/).
