Keng Seng Choo is the subject of **“终身的事业，幸福的意义 — 油画家龚明金”**, a chapter in **心路2 — 走向大马艺术家**, the interview collection by **朵拉**.

Published by **Soka Gakkai Malaysia in 2011**, the book brings together conversations with Malaysian artists. Its ISBN is **978-967-1046-51-7**. Publication details and the chapter listing are available on the publisher’s website.
