Taipei Fine Arts Museum’s official archive lists Keng Seng Choo, under his Chinese name 龔明金, among the exhibiting artists in **The 2nd Exhibition of the Asian Federation of Watercolor** (亞洲水彩聯盟第二屆展).

The group exhibition ran from **14 November to 16 December 1987**, in galleries 201–209. This museum record documents an early international exhibition in his career. It does not identify an individual painting or establish a museum acquisition.

## 中文

台北市立美术馆的官方展览档案记载，龔明金参与了1987年11月14日至12月16日举行的「亞洲水彩聯盟第二屆展」，展场为201至209展间。这份记录为其早期国际参展经历提供了机构出处。
