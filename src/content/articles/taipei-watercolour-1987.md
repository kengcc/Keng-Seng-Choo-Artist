Keng Seng Choo exhibited in **The 2nd Exhibition of the Asian Federation of Watercolor** (亞洲水彩聯盟第二屆展) at **Taipei Fine Arts Museum** from **14 November to 16 December 1987**.

Held in galleries **201–209**, the exhibition forms part of his early international exhibition history. The museum’s exhibition archive lists him under his Chinese name **龔明金**.

## 中文

龚明金参与了1987年11月14日至12月16日在台北市立美术馆举行的「亞洲水彩聯盟第二屆展」，展场为201至209展间。

这项展览是他早期国际参展经历的一部分。美术馆展览档案以「龔明金」列出他的姓名。
