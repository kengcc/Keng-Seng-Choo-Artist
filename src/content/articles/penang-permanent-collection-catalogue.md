新加坡国家美术馆的 Rotunda Library & Archive 保存了一项与龚明金有关的书目记录：<strong>《Penang State Art Gallery permanent collection 1965–2011》</strong>，由 <strong>Tan Chee Khuan</strong> 编辑，共<strong>304页</strong>，ISBN <strong>9789834237677</strong>。

该书目展示的内容目录，在 <strong>Works of Malaysian Artists</strong> 一节列出 <strong>Keng Seng Choo</strong>。书目附注说明，这本图录记录了槟州美术馆于1965至2011年间收藏的578件作品。

此处核实的是图录的书目和目录记录。网页未列出龚明金作品的具体画名、购藏年份或尺寸，也不代表新加坡国家美术馆收藏了该画作；新加坡的机构是这本参考书的馆藏地点。

## English

National Gallery Singapore’s library catalogue lists Keng Seng Choo in the contents of <strong>Penang State Art Gallery permanent collection 1965–2011</strong>, edited by Tan Chee Khuan, <strong>304 pages</strong>, ISBN <strong>9789834237677</strong>. This verifies a bibliographic reference to the artist in the Penang collection catalogue. It does not identify an individual work or establish ownership of his paintings by National Gallery Singapore.
