龚明金收录于<strong>《Penang State Art Gallery permanent collection 1965–2011》</strong>的 <strong>Works of Malaysian Artists</strong> 一节。

这本槟州美术馆典藏图录由 <strong>Tan Chee Khuan</strong> 编辑，共<strong>304页</strong>，介绍美术馆于1965至2011年间收藏的578件作品。ISBN 为 <strong>9789834237677</strong>。

图录可于<strong>新加坡国家美术馆 Rotunda Library & Archive</strong>的馆藏书目中查阅。

## English

Keng Seng Choo is included in the <strong>Works of Malaysian Artists</strong> section of <strong>Penang State Art Gallery permanent collection 1965–2011</strong>.

Edited by <strong>Tan Chee Khuan</strong>, the <strong>304-page</strong> catalogue covers 578 works collected by the Penang gallery between 1965 and 2011. ISBN: <strong>9789834237677</strong>.

The book is held by <strong>National Gallery Singapore’s Rotunda Library & Archive</strong>, whose library catalogue provides the publication details and contents.
