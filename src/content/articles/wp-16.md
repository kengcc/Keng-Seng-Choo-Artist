13 Sep 2009, Sun ([Art Loft Gallery](https://artloftgallery.blogspot.com/2009/09/introducing-keng-seng-choo.html "Art Loft Gallery featuring Keng Seng Choo")): A senior artist who was born in Kedah in 1945. He was educated at Nanyang Academy of Fine Arts, Singapore. He had frequently participated in art exhibitions in Singapore and Malaysia, and won The New York International Art Show (Silver Medal).

I really enjoy his work. Was browsing on the net yesterday when I came across a [Bank Negara (Malaysia Central Bank) website](https://artcentre.bnm.gov.my/index.php?ch=14&pg=56&ac=214 "Keng Seng Choo's Painting in Bank Negara") of their collection of his painting.
