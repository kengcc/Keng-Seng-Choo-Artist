Seng Choo’s painting of nubile rural women with angular chins and slightly elongated bodies, evoke’s the serene abandon of Soo-Pieng's works. Contemplation has more to do with the romantic sense of loss yearning for the unattainable. It is a sensuous piece of art in which a landscape of inhospitable grandeur keeps melting into colours, forms and textures of flesh.
