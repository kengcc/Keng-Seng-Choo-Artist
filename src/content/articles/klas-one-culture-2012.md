**One Culture: Two Perspectives** brought together Keng Seng Choo and Goh Kwan Chin at KL Lifestyle Art Space in Bangsar from **8 August to 9 September 2012**.

The KLAS feature describes a presentation of 100 works exploring village life and Balinese subjects through the two artists’ different approaches. It discusses Keng Seng Choo’s pigeon and figurative paintings alongside Goh Kwan Chin’s batik practice.

The dates come from the exhibition feature itself, rather than the later upload date of its PDF. Younie Gallery’s artist biography also records the two-artist exhibition.
