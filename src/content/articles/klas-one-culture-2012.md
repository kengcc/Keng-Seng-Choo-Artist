**One Culture: Two Perspectives** brought together Keng Seng Choo and Goh Kwan Chin at KL Lifestyle Art Space in Bangsar from **8 August to 9 September 2012**.

The exhibition presented 100 works exploring village life and Balinese subjects through the two artists’ different approaches. KLAS’s exhibition feature discusses Keng Seng Choo’s pigeon and figurative paintings alongside Goh Kwan Chin’s batik practice.

## 中文

2012年8月8日至9月9日，龚明金与 Goh Kwan Chin 在吉隆坡孟沙 KL Lifestyle Art Space 举办「One Culture: Two Perspectives」双人展。

展览汇集100件作品，以两位艺术家各自的表现手法描绘乡村生活与峇厘题材。KLAS 的展览介绍亦讨论了龚明金的鸽子与人物绘画，以及 Goh Kwan Chin 的蜡染创作。
